What it does

Everything you check before a set, in one window

TrackDeck is a player, not a library manager. Drop in a folder, and the information you care about is right there.

🌈

RGB waveform

The waveform is coloured by frequency: bass red, mids green, highs blue. Breakdowns, drops and vocals stand out before you press play.

📊

Live visualiserNew

A real-time spectrum next to the track info, timed to what you hear. Bars, LED segments, mirrored or a smooth curve.

🥁

BPM detectionNew

No BPM tag? TrackDeck measures the tempo the first time you play a track and remembers it. Long DJ mixes are skipped.

🎹

Sort by keyNew

Click a column to sort. Keys follow the Camelot wheel, so 8A sits next to 9A, and Camelot, Open Key and "Am" all work.

🏷️

Columns from your tags

Artist, Title, Album, Genre, Key, Label, BPM, Year, Bitrate and Duration. Pick the columns you want and drag them into order.

🎨

Make it yours

A dark Modern skin and a teal Khoshekh skin, an accent colour, waveform colour schemes, and your album art as the window background.

🎧

Plays everything

MP3, AAC, Apple Lossless, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg, Opus, WMA and more, with gapless playback. Media keys and Now Playing on Mac.

⚡

Native and light

A small C++ app, not a web page in a box. The visualiser uses about 7% of one CPU core on an Apple Silicon Mac.

Visualiser

Four styles, your colours

Choose the style, bar density, sensitivity, treble boost, fall speed and frame rate in Preferences. Colours follow the waveform, your accent colour, or a gradient you pick.

Plan your set

Sort by key, BPM or anything else

  • Click a header to sort, click again to reverse, and a third time to put your order back.
  • Keys sort on the Camelot wheel, so harmonically compatible tracks end up together.
  • BPM, Year and Bitrate sort as numbers and Duration as time. Empty cells go last.
  • Playback follows the order you see, and it's saved with your playlist.

Skins

Modern, or Khoshekh

  • Modern: dark, clean and the default, in your choice of accent colour.
  • Khoshekh: the same layout in deep teal and cream.
  • Album art background: the current cover, blurred, fills the window.
  • Classic skins from Nulloy (Metro, Silver, Slim) are still there too.

Download

Free for macOS and Windows

No installer wizard and no account. Your settings and playlist carry over between versions.

macOS

Apple Silicon (M1 and later) · macOS 14 Sonoma or newer

Download .dmg
  1. Open the .dmg and drag TrackDeck to Applications.
  2. The app isn't notarized yet, so the first time, right-click it and choose Open. Or run: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TrackDeck.app

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 · x64 · portable, no installer

Download .zip
  1. Unzip anywhere and run TrackDeck\TrackDeck.exe.
  2. If SmartScreen warns about an unrecognised app, choose More info → Run anyway.
